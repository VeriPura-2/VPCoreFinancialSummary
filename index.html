The product: built today, and what comes next.

The €2M scales an engine that already works

More than 70 orchestrated AI agents on one shared engine, at a 94% gross margin. CORE has already generated revenue in project work VeriPura delivered outside the food trials.

● Commercially live

  • Rapid extraction from every document format
  • Patent-pending checks (U.S. provisional 64/088,697): every document checked for validity, authenticity, relevance, completeness and agreement across the set
  • Missing papers chased automatically by email, public registers and messaging
  • Regulatory coverage: 15 national tariff schedules and 17 live regulatory feeds, including FDA and EU RASFF
  • Compliance modules: UK packaging EPR (all 12,069 producers), halal certifiers in 5 countries
  • Consignment Passport: QR-verifiable by port authorities

● In testing

  • Sanctions screening (OFAC, EU, UN, UK)
  • EUDR deforestation checks, read from the regulation
  • EU green claims audit (rules in force since September 2026)

● Planned

  • EU Forced Labour Regulation (applies December 2027)
  • Confidence score on every check

Food and cosmetics in the seed period; pharma, supplements, apparel and the US funded by growth

One engine, 100% shared across categories: each new category adds domain experts and rules, not new software. Growth hiring, US expansion and marketing step-ups wait for ARR to pass €3M (month 18) and €6M (month 21).

Agentic efficiency, and the team.

Year 3 EBITDA margin vs listed peers

Adjusted EBITDA margin, latest fiscal year. Peers carry legacy organisations sized for manual work. CORE's 70+ agents run the checks and rule research; company agents run bookkeeping, development, testing, lead generation and social media. So product and engineering run at 15% of revenue and selling and administration at 20%, against 20% to 25% and 30% to 40% for typical SaaS.

Revenue per employee vs listed peers

US$ thousands per full-time equivalent. VeriPura: month 36 run rate (ARR €79M across about 198 FTE, at €1 = $1.15), counting contract and top-up spend as people. Peers: latest annual revenue over reported headcount. Legacy players are now cutting to get here: WiseTech plans to remove about 2,000 roles in product, development and customer service by FY27.

Headcount grows with categories and partners, not with checks

Full-time equivalents at each quarter end, base case: core roles plus contract and top-up spend converted to people. Headcount stays under 25 until ARR passes €4M; ARR per FTE rises from €94k at month 12 to €399k at month 36.
